Runs a full-INT8 Wav2Lip on your CPU, multithreaded (up to 4 threads on phones). The model is about 37 MB and downloaded once.

Source: wav2lip-tflite. LiteRT.js runs Wav2Lip, MediaPipe BlazeFace finds faces, Mediabunny writes the MP4.
